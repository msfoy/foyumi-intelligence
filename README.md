# FOYUMI Intelligence

## Interactive Strategic Prototype

FOYUMI Intelligence is an academic proof-of-concept demonstrating
how operational business data can be transformed into customer,
service, booking and demand intelligence.

### Strategic Intervention

The prototype demonstrates the proposed:

**FOYUMI Customer & Booking Intelligence System**

Architecture:

Raw Data
→ Cleaning & Governance
→ Integration
→ Customer Intelligence
→ Service Intelligence
→ Statistical Demand Forecasting
→ Management Action

### Intelligence Areas

- Business performance intelligence
- Customer RFM segmentation
- Customer value analysis
- Service intelligence
- Home-service operational coverage
- Booking-entry intelligence
- Statistical booking-demand forecasting
- Structured booking data capture

### Forecasting

The demand forecast uses completed booking volume rather than
revenue.

Revenue was intentionally excluded as the predictive target because
service prices can change independently of booking volume.

The Q4 2026 forecast is an operational planning baseline and is not
a guarantee of future demand.

### Data Protection

The public prototype does not publish the underlying customer-level
source datasets. Only aggregated analytical outputs required for
the demonstration are included.

### Prototype Status

This is an academic strategic proof-of-concept.

It is not a production booking platform.
